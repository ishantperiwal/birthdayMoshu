# Male expression decals

The male Kiss and Amazed expressions use dedicated 386 × 329 transparent PNGs generated by editing the original `faceDecalguy.png` artwork:

- `../manualAssets/faceDecalguy-kiss.png`
- `../manualAssets/faceDecalguy-amazed.png`

Both retain the original warm, softly textured toy-print style and UV alignment. Kiss uses closed eyelids and a compact brick-brown pucker. Amazed uses raised brows, slightly larger highlighted eyes, and a compact delighted open mouth. Normal, Sad, and natural blink keep the existing rendering path. The stable network IDs remain `happy` and `surprised` for saved-client compatibility.

The built-in image-generation edit workflow was used with `faceDecalguy.png` as the edit target. Final prompts required exact canvas alignment, transparent background, no head or skin, no extra features, and tightly constrained mouth proportions so both expressions remain readable at gameplay scale.
